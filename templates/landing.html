System Architecture
ACID Transactions & Locking

Order execution runs inside InnoDB transactions using START TRANSACTION, SELECT ... FOR UPDATE row locks, and COMMIT / ROLLBACK semantics.

Paper Trading Wallet

Simulated cash wallet enforcing non-negative balance constraints. Automatic weighted average purchase price calculation on buy orders.

Relational Normalization (3NF)

Decomposed across 10 relational tables eliminating insertion, update, and deletion anomalies with verified foreign key integrity.

Chart.js Visual Analytics

Real-time sector weightage donut charts, asset allocation bar charts, and historical price trend lines rendered dynamically.

Automated Triggers & Audits

Database triggers maintain price history ticks, prevent negative balances, and log state changes into the Audit_Log table.

Financial Statement Exports

Generate and download 6 financial reports including Complete Transactions, BUY/SELL Orders, Portfolio Holdings, and Stock-Wise Investment in CSV and printable formats.
