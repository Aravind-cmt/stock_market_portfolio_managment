Core Architecture & Capabilities

ACID Transactions & Row Locking

Order execution operates under strict MySQL InnoDB ACID transactions using START TRANSACTION, SELECT ... FOR UPDATE row locks, and automated COMMIT / ROLLBACK.

Simulated Paper Wallet

Every user receives ₹1,00,000 in paper trading balance to practice buying and selling without risking capital, validated with DB check constraints.

3NF Normalized Relational Schema

Engineered across 9 tables adhering strictly to 1NF, 2NF, and 3NF, eliminating partial dependencies and update anomalies.

Visual Analytics & Chart.js

Dynamic sector and asset allocation donut charts, along with responsive historical stock price lines and trend indicators.

Automated Triggers & Audits

Every trade and administrative action is preserved into the Audit_Log table, with triggers ensuring non-negative balances.

CSV & Printable Reports

Instant one-click exports for Complete Transactions, Buys, Sells, Holdings, and Profit/Loss reports with print media support.
